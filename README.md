# 🚗 Car Rental UI/UX Case Study  

## 📌 Overview  
This repository showcases the **UI/UX case study of Car Rental Platform**, a modern car rental platform designed to redefine user experience in the mobility sector.  

The case study highlights the **design process, research findings, visual direction, and final prototypes** used to create a seamless digital experience for users.  

🔗 **Live Case Study on Behance:**  
[SoftCar – Redefining Car Rental UI/UX](https://www.behance.net/gallery/216605583/SoftCar-Redefining-Car-Rental-UIUX-Case-Study)  

---

## 🎯 Deliverables  
- ✅ **Case Study Documentation** – Design process & insights  
- ✅ **User Experience Research** – Personas, flows, and wireframes  
- ✅ **High-Fidelity UI Screens** – Desktop & mobile interfaces  
- ✅ **Testimonials** – Client/peer feedback on design outcome
